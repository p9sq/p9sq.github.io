ABOUT TIRA

Terranian Interplanetary Research Authority

TIRA is Terrania's foremost scientific institution, charged with the exploration and study of the cosmos beyond the Phebe System. Its public mandate spans deep-space telescope networks, advanced propulsion research, long-baseline observatories, and crewed exploration programmes. Founded in the early centuries of the Terranian space age, TIRA has remained at the forefront of interstellar discovery for over a millennium.

◆
ABOUT EXOPLANET CORPORATION

Exoplanet Corporation

A semi-independent subsidiary of TIRA, the Exoplanet Corporation operates gravitational-lensing observatories stationed hundreds of AU from the Phebe System's barycentre, achieving kilometre-scale resolution of distant planetary systems. Its singular mission: locate a second Terrania — a world capable of supporting Terranian civilisation — before crewed interstellar travel matures into widespread practice.

The Planetary Archive is the Exoplanet Corporation's public-facing catalogue of all known exoplanetary systems and colonisation candidates.

◉
THE TERRANIA 2.0 MISSION

Finding a Second Home

With interstellar travel approaching feasibility, the search for a world that can serve as a second cradle for Terranian life has never been more urgent. The Terrania 2.0 programme catalogues promising planetary candidates by habitability index, atmospheric composition, and biosignature potential — ranking the most viable destinations for future colonisation efforts.
