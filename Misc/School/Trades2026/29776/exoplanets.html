Types of Exoplanets

Astronomers sort exoplanets into loose groups based on their size, what they're made of, and how they orbit. Unlike the Solar System's clean split into rocky and giant planets, these groups often overlap, and they're still being worked out as more planets are found. Pick a type below to read more.

  • Hot Jupiters
  • Super-Earths
  • Mini-Neptunes
  • Ocean Worlds
  • Lava Worlds
  • Exo-Ice Giants
  • Rogue Planets
  • Earth Analogs

There's also a page about the recently reported CD-35 2722 B exosatellite, a strange object that doesn't fit neatly into any of the groups above.
