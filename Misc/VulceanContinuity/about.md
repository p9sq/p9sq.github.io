# About the Vulcean Continuity

The Vulcean Continuity is a worldbuilding project I started in early 2025. This project takes place on a fictional Earth-like planet (Arventia) in a fictional star system (Athovon System), which is located roughly 47.99 thousand light-years away from Earth on the opposite side of the Milky Way in the constellation of Scorpius. This is also my first ever worldbuilding project, meaning that I may not be the best at this kind of thing 😅 (The main reason I started this project was because I had a story which I had created back in 2022 or so, and as of early 2025, I got into astronomy, and then when I found out about worldbuilding, I had decided to create the Vulcean Continuity)

### Namesake

The Vulcean Continuity is named after the hypothetical planet Vulcan that was thought to exist between the Sun and Mercury (which actually exists canonically in my lore, as well as Planet X), which has been disproven (in real life) by general relativity.

# About Habitable Worlds

Habitable Worlds is a public website catalogue which aims to let the public (Arventian inhabitants) know about potential candidate Arventia 2.0 analogues that have been discovered. Habitable Worlds was also developed by AIRA (Arventian Interplanetary Research Authority, which is the NASA analogue of my worldbuilding project) and Exoplanet Corporation, which is a sub-company led by a group of AIRA members.

# Lore (Vulcean Continuity)

The Vulcean Continuity takes place in the year 3000 (Earth years) and near the end of the year 3307 (Arventian years, or the year 2999 in Earth years). This difference in years happens because an Arventian year is about 9.31% shorter than an Earth year.

Earth’s technology has advanced significantly over many centuries, especially in exoplanet science, with many new exoplanets and even exomoons having been confirmed.

The Athovon System, the home stellar system of the main civilisation in this story (Arventians, who live on the Earth-like planet known as Arventia), sits on the opposite side of the Milky Way from the Sun at roughly 47.99 thousand light years in the Scorpius constellation. The system’s primary star is a spectral type G7 main-sequence yellow dwarf star called Athovon, and the secondary star of the system is called Vexulan, which is a smaller red dwarf separated from Athovon by roughly 4,144 AU. This system includes 12 major planets, ten dwarf planets, and many asteroids and comets.

Both Humans and Arventians have no idea of each other's existence due to the Milky Way’s thick dust and gas getting in the way of detection.
