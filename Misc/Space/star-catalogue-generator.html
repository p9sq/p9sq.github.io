Star Catalogue Generator

Builds a Space Engine CSV star catalogue. Covers main sequence dwarfs, giants and supergiants, Wolf-Rayet stars, white dwarfs, neutron stars, black holes, and brown dwarfs, all with mass, radius, and temperature tied together by the same physical models, age included where age changes the numbers.

Star names come out as name + separator + number, for example GEN 1, GEN 2, GEN 3.

Added to every star's distance. Use this to place the whole catalogue somewhere other than right next to the Sun.

Distances are sampled on a log scale between these two, since the default range spans nearby stars to nearby galaxies. RA and dec are sampled uniformly over the whole sky. Stars are kept at least a light-year apart from each other so they don't pile up like a cluster.
