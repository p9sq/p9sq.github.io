Star Catalogue Generator

Builds a Space Engine CSV star catalogue. Mass, radius, temperature, and apparent magnitude are all derived from the same physical model, so they stay consistent with each other.

Star names come out as name + separator + number, zero-padded to match your star count so they sort cleanly in Space Engine. Leave a field blank to use its default, shown as the greyed-out placeholder.

Added to every star's distance. Use this to place the whole catalogue somewhere other than right next to the Sun.

Distances are sampled on a log scale between these two, since the default range spans nearby stars to nearby galaxies. RA and dec are sampled uniformly over the whole sky.
