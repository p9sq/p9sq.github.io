ABOUT AIRA

Arventian Interplanetary Research Authority

AIRA is Arventia's foremost scientific institution, charged with the exploration and study of the cosmos beyond the Phebe System. Its public mandate spans deep-space telescope networks, advanced propulsion research, long-baseline observatories, and crewed exploration programmes. Founded in the early centuries of the Arventian space age, AIRA has remained at the forefront of interstellar discovery for over a millennium.

ABOUT EXOPLANET CORPORATION

Exoplanet Corporation

A semi-independent subsidiary of AIRA, the Exoplanet Corporation operates gravitational-lensing observatories stationed hundreds of AU from the Phebe System's barycentre, achieving kilometre-scale resolution of distant planetary systems. Its singular mission: locate a second Arventia — a world capable of supporting Arventian civilisation — before crewed interstellar travel matures into widespread practice. The Planetary Archive is the Exoplanet Corporation's public-facing catalogue of all known exoplanetary systems and colonisation candidates.

THE ARVENTIA 2.0 MISSION

Finding a Second Home

With interstellar travel approaching feasibility, the search for a world that can serve as a second cradle for Arventian life has never been more urgent. The Arventia 2.0 programme catalogues promising planetary candidates by habitability index, atmospheric composition, and biosignature potential — ranking the most viable destinations for future colonisation efforts.
