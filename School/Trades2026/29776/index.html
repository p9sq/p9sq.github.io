ABOUT AIRA

Arventian Interplanetary Research Authority

AIRA leads Arventia's science and exploration beyond the Phebe System. AIRA runs deep-space telescope networks, propulsion research, long-baseline observatories, and crewed exploration programmes. AIRA started in the early centuries of the Arventian space age and has led interstellar discovery for over a thousand years.

  • Deep-space telescope networks
  • Advanced propulsion research
  • Long-baseline observatories
  • Crewed exploration programmes

ABOUT EXOPLANET CORPORATION

Exoplanet Corporation

The Exoplanet Corporation is a semi-independent subsidiary of AIRA. The Corporation operates gravitational-lensing observatories hundreds of AU from the Phebe System's barycentre. These observatories reach kilometre-scale resolution on distant planetary systems. The Corporation's mission is simple: find a second Arventia, a world able to support Arventian life, before crewed interstellar travel becomes common.

The Planetary Archive is the Exoplanet Corporation's public catalogue of every known exoplanetary system and colonisation candidate. Check real exoplanet data at the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

THE ARVENTIA 2.0 MISSION

Finding a Second Home

Interstellar travel is getting closer. Arventia needs a second home for the Arventian people. The Arventia 2.0 programme tracks promising planetary candidates by habitability index, atmospheric composition, and biosignature potential. The programme ranks these candidates by how viable they are for future colonisation.
